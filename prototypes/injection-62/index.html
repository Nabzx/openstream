Prototype - throwaway - issue #62

Where does the dictated text actually land?

You hold a hotkey, talk, and let go. Somewhere in the next fraction of a second the app has to put your words into the box you were pointing at. This demo lets you drive that moment by hand: choose which app is in front, whether it is a normal text box or a terminal, whether you just switched apps a moment ago, whether the app answers questions at all - and then watch which delivery method gets picked, whether the text arrives, and what happens to your clipboard. The question it exists to settle: one delivery method or a chain of fallbacks, what stops us delivering to the app the user has just left, and what the user sees when delivery fails.
